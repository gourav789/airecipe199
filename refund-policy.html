Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

airecipe.onl is a digital tool where access is granted immediately after payment. Please read this Refund Policy carefully before purchasing.

1. About the digital product

This is a one-time ₹199 digital access. Because you get full access to the tool immediately after payment, the product is "instant digital access" in nature.

2. When you are eligible for a refund

We want you to be satisfied. A refund will be provided in the following cases:

  • You were charged twice (duplicate payment) — the extra payment will be fully refunded.
  • The payment went through but, due to a technical issue, you received no access at all, and we were unable to fix it.
  • You were charged by mistake even though you did not make the purchase.

3. When a refund will not apply

  • If you have already received access to the tool and it is working properly.
  • Simply a "change of mind" after access has been granted.
  • If the AI-generated recipes did not meet your expectations (since these are AI suggestions and can differ each time).
⚠️ Since this is instant digital access, refunds are only provided in the special cases mentioned above.

4. How to request a refund

To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of payment through our Contact page with the following details:

  • Your registered email
  • Razorpay payment / order ID
  • A short description of the problem

5. Refund processing time

Once a refund is approved, the amount will be returned to your original payment method within 5–7 working days. This timing depends on your bank / Razorpay.

6. Contact

For any help related to payments or refunds, write to us on our Contact page — we'll help you quickly.
