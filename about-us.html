About Us

AI-powered recipes from the ingredients you already have — no stress.

What we do

airecipe.onl is an AI-powered recipe tool that solves a very common everyday problem — "I have ingredients at home, but I can't decide what to cook today."

You simply select whatever is available in your kitchen — vegetables, fruits, spices, oils, packaged items (like oats or instant noodles), non-veg items, and whether or not you have an oven. Our AI then instantly creates 5 delicious recipes that you can make right away using exactly those ingredients.

How the idea started

Almost every home faces the same daily question — "what should I cook today?" The fridge is open, ingredients are there, but nothing comes to mind, and no one feels like running to the market. We built airecipe.onl to make this daily struggle easier, so you can create something new and tasty from what you already have.

Our mission

To make cooking easy for everyone. Whether you are experienced or a complete beginner, our recipes come with simple, step-by-step instructions so anyone can follow along. It also helps reduce food waste, because you make full use of the ingredients already in your kitchen.

💡 We believe you don't need 50 ingredients to cook something good. You just need a little guidance — and that's exactly what our AI provides.
